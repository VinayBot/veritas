# Vacation Policy

Employees accrue two paid vacation days for every month of continuous service, up to a maximum
of twenty-four days per calendar year. Requests must be submitted through the HR portal at
least two weeks before the intended start date, and a manager must approve the request before
it is considered confirmed. Unused vacation days roll over into the following year, but the
rollover balance is capped at ten days; anything beyond that cap is forfeited at the end of the
calendar year unless local law requires otherwise. Part-time employees accrue vacation on a
pro-rated basis according to their contracted weekly hours. Employees on an approved leave of
absence continue to accrue vacation at half the normal rate for the duration of the leave,
resuming the full rate immediately upon return.

# Expense Reimbursement

Employees must submit expense reports within thirty days of the original purchase date to be
eligible for reimbursement. Reports that include a single line item over five hundred dollars
require written approval from the employee's manager before the report can be submitted to
finance. All reports must include itemized receipts; credit card statements alone are not
sufficient documentation. International travel expenses must be converted to the home currency
using the exchange rate in effect on the date of purchase, not the date of submission. Mileage
for personal vehicle use is reimbursed at the standard rate published annually by finance, and
parking or toll receipts should be attached separately rather than bundled into a single
scanned image.

# Remote Work Guidelines

Employees may work remotely for up to three days per week, subject to manager approval and the
needs of their specific team. Equipment requests, including monitors, keyboards, and docking
stations, are submitted through the IT helpdesk portal and typically ship within five business
days. Remote employees are expected to be reachable during their team's core collaboration
hours and to attend any meetings that are designated as in-person-required at least one week in
advance. Home office stipends of a fixed annual amount are available to any employee who works
remotely at least one day per week on a regular basis. Teams that are fully distributed, with
no members sharing a primary office, may request an exception to the three-day cap through
their department head.

# Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and vendors with respect and
professionalism in every interaction. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation of any kind
are strictly prohibited and will be investigated promptly by the People team. Employees who
witness or experience a violation of this policy are encouraged to report it through the
confidential ethics hotline, which is staffed by an independent third party and available
around the clock. Conflicts of interest, including outside employment, significant financial
stakes in a vendor or competitor, or close personal relationships with someone in a reporting
line, must be disclosed to HR as soon as they arise.

# Parental Leave

Employees who have completed at least six months of continuous service are eligible for
sixteen weeks of paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or fostering of a child.
Leave may be taken in a single continuous block or split into two blocks within the first year,
subject to manager approval for the split arrangement. Health benefits continue uninterrupted
during parental leave at the same cost-sharing arrangement as active employment, and employees
returning from parental leave are guaranteed a role at the same level and comparable
compensation to the one they left. A phased return, at reduced hours for up to four weeks, is
available on request.

# Performance Reviews

Formal performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in the first weeks of January and July.
Each review includes a self-assessment, a manager assessment, and a calibration discussion
among managers to ensure consistency across teams. Ratings are assigned on a five-point scale,
and any rating at the bottom two points automatically triggers a structured improvement plan
with explicit milestones reviewed every thirty days. Peer feedback is collected confidentially
from at least three colleagues chosen by the employee and two chosen by the manager.

# Equipment and IT Security

Company-issued laptops must have full-disk encryption and the standard endpoint security agent
installed before they leave the IT department, and employees may not disable either without
written approval from security. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT helpdesk
within twenty-four hours so the device can be remotely locked and wiped. Multi-factor
authentication is mandatory for every system that handles customer data, and shared accounts
without individual attribution are not permitted under any circumstances.

# Travel and Safety

Business travel to any location on the company's restricted-travel list requires pre-approval
from both the employee's manager and the security team, regardless of trip duration. Employees
traveling internationally are automatically enrolled in a travel-tracking service that can
locate and assist them in the event of a natural disaster, civil unrest, or medical emergency.
All business travel must be booked through the approved travel portal so that the company can
maintain accurate records of employee location for safety purposes.

# Learning and Development

Every employee has an annual learning stipend that can be used for conference tickets, online
courses, certification exams, or job-related books, and unused funds do not roll over into the
following year. Internal mobility is encouraged, and employees who have been in their current
role for at least a year may apply to posted internal openings without needing their current
manager's prior approval, though the manager is notified once an interview is scheduled. A
mentorship program pairs new hires with a volunteer mentor outside their immediate team for
their first six months.

# Workplace Health and Safety

Every office maintains a stocked first-aid kit and at least two employees trained in basic
first aid and CPR on each floor during business hours. Any workplace injury, regardless of
severity, must be reported to a manager and to facilities within twenty-four hours so an
incident report can be filed. Fire drills are conducted twice a year without advance notice,
and floor wardens are responsible for confirming their area is clear before reporting to the
designated assembly point.

# Confidentiality and Data Handling

Employees must not share confidential business information, including unreleased product
plans, financial results, or customer data, outside the company without explicit written
authorization. Customer data accessed for support or analysis purposes may only be used for the
specific purpose it was accessed for and must never be exported to a personal device or
personal cloud storage account. Confidentiality obligations continue after an employee leaves
the company and survive the termination of employment indefinitely for trade secrets. Any
suspected data breach, however small, must be reported to the security team immediately.
